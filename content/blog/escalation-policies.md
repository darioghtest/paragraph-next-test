Every team escalates a little differently. Some page the primary, then the secondary, then the manager. Others page a whole rotation at once for anything marked critical. Until now, Relay only supported the first pattern.

## What's new

- **Severity rules.** Route critical alerts straight to a group, and let low-severity ones wait for business hours.
- **Service overrides.** Give the payments service its own chain without copying the rest of the policy.
- **Preview.** Pick a time and an alert, and Relay shows exactly who would be paged, in what order.

## Getting started

Open **Settings → Escalation** and pick a policy to edit. Existing policies keep working as they are.
